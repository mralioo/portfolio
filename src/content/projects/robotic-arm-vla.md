My current side build, bridging foundation models and the physical world.

- **Hardware:** arm mechanics, servo actuation, sensors and cameras, power electronics.
- **Embedded:** microcontroller firmware, motor control loops, on-device inference.
- **Learning:** collecting teleoperated demonstrations, training vision-language-action (VLA)
  policies with imitation learning, then fine-tuning with reinforcement learning and
  testing sim-to-real transfer.

Write-ups, videos, and the repo will land here as milestones ship.
